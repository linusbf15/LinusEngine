scons platform=windows dev_build=no d3d12=no angle=no accesskit=no winrt=no target=editor lto=none module_mono_enabled=yes -j8
Debug:
scons platform=windows dev_build=no d3d12=yes angle=yes production=yes accesskit=yes winrt=yes target=editor lto=none module_mono_enabled=yes -j8
Full Debug:
scons platform=windows dev_build=yes d3d12=yes angle=yes production=no accesskit=yes winrt=yes target=editor lto=none module_mono_enabled=yes -j8

scons platform=windows target=template_release module_mono_enabled=yes -j8
